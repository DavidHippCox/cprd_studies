flowchart TD
    A[Alcohol observation] --> B{Medcode in alcohol_unit lookup?}

    B -->|No| X[Excluded from candidate set]
    B -->|Yes| C{Acceptable numunitid present?}

    C -->|No| R1[REJECT<br/>NUMUNIT_NULL or NUMUNIT_NOT_ACCEPTED]
    C -->|Yes| D{Unit and period established<br/>without conflict?}

    D -->|No| R2[REJECT<br/>UNIT or PERIOD reason]
    D -->|Yes| E{amount_type}

    E -->|VALUE| F{Value present?}
    F -->|No| R3[REJECT<br/>VALUE_NULL]
    F -->|Yes| G[Use observation value]

    E -->|SCALAR| H{Value present?}
    H -->|No| I[Use numunit scalar]
    H -->|Yes| J{Value equals scalar?}
    J -->|No| R4[REJECT<br/>VALUE_NOT_EQUAL_TO_NUMUNIT]
    J -->|Yes| G

    E -->|RANGE| K{Value present?}
    K -->|No| L[Use numunit lower and upper bounds]
    K -->|Yes| M{Value within inclusive range?}
    M -->|No| R5[REJECT<br/>VALUE_OUTSIDE_NUMUNIT_RANGE]
    M -->|Yes| G

    G --> N[Resolve exact lower and upper amount]
    I --> N
    L --> N

    N --> O{Valid multiplier and amount?}

    O -->|No| R6[REJECT<br/>CONVERSION, NEGATIVE,<br/>or ZERO_NOT_ALLOWED]
    O -->|Yes| P[ACCEPT]

    P --> Q[Convert lower and upper amounts<br/>to weekly alcohol units]
